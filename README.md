# Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line

One sentence on a small moment of wonder in everyday life:

> A stranger's umbrella tilted over my shoulder at the bus stop, and for one block the rain had manners.

A stranger shares their umbrella at a bus stop, and for one block the rain behaves like a polite guest — a small kindness that makes the everyday strange and new.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt` when run.
